Turn long videos into short clips

ClipForge is a Windows desktop app that finds the strongest moments in long videos and livestream recordings, and edits them into captioned vertical clips for TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

What it does

  • Finds highlight moments in long videos using the audio and a speech transcript.
  • Edits each moment into a 9:16 vertical clip with word-by-word captions and a credit to the original creator.
  • Shows trends from public YouTube and Twitch data to help decide what to make.

How it works with TikTok

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, ClipForge uses TikTok's official APIs to:

  • show the account's basic profile and statistics (followers, likes, video count) and the view, like, comment and share counts of the account's public videos;
  • send a finished clip to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft, which the owner reviews and posts themselves in the TikTok app.

ClipForge runs entirely on the owner's computer. There is no ClipForge server or account, and TikTok data is never sold. Data is sent to another service only when a feature needs it, as set out in the Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time with ClipForge's Disconnect button or in TikTok's app-permission settings.

Who runs ClipForge? ClipForge is a personal tool built and used by an independent creator (TikTok @clip_rr1) to publish their own clips. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok.
